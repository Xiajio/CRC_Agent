# Product

## Register

product

## Users

LangG serves three connected groups: patients completing CRC triage, clinicians reviewing evidence-backed drafts, and internal operators or principal investigators supervising the agent, research assets, safety gates, and releases. The Agent Admin research console is primarily for trained internal operators, research staff, evidence reviewers, and release managers working in an authenticated operational environment.

## Product Purpose

LangG is a colorectal-cancer clinical decision-support system that combines patient intake, clinician assistance, evidence retrieval, and agent operations. Its research surface helps authorized users inspect literature evidence, assess cohort-data readiness, and create reviewable learning candidates without treating unreviewed outputs as clinical facts or silently changing patient or doctor workflows. Success means every research artifact has an honest runtime state, traceable provenance, explicit governance boundaries, and a clear next review action.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, accountable. The product speaks directly, distinguishes observed runtime data from catalog or roadmap content, and favors clinical and operational clarity over promotional language.

## Anti-references

- Marketing-style SaaS dashboards that exaggerate autonomy or readiness.
- Saturated red command centers where every element competes for urgency.
- Fake pipelines, invented metrics, or catalog entries presented as live runtime data.
- Interfaces that imply literature candidates are approved clinical evidence.
- Hidden side effects, automatic promotion, or ambiguous buttons that can change prompts, RAG, policy, training data, or clinical paths.

## Design Principles

1. Show the source and state of every consequential value.
2. Keep clinical, research, and release-governance boundaries visible at the point of action.
3. Prefer dense, scannable operational layouts with progressive detail over decorative cards.
4. Make mutations explicit, narrow, idempotent, and followed by a refreshed observable result.
5. Reuse the established component, token, and status vocabulary so users can transfer trust between admin surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All primary workflows must be keyboard operable, preserve visible focus, use semantic labels and status text, and avoid encoding state by color alone. Text and controls should maintain readable contrast in each workspace theme. Motion must communicate state, respect reduced-motion preferences, and never gate access to content. Dense data views must remain usable at narrow widths through structural reflow rather than reduced text size.
