# Intended Use Boundaries

## Patient CRC Triage

Patient CRC Triage supports symptom collection, triage-support explanation, preparation of information for clinician review, and urgent or emergency safety messaging. It must not provide a final diagnosis, make treatment decisions, conclude screening outcomes, reassure red flags as benign, or replace clinician review.

## Doctor Review

Doctor Review supports clinician-facing summaries, draft review notes, and evidence provenance display. It is assistive only: it must not auto-sign, override clinician decisions, or convert clinician edits into model truth.

## Research Workspace

Research Workspace supports literature radar, cohort-feasibility exploration, and hypothesis drafting for research users. It must not give patient advice, make clinical decisions, or export patient-level data without review.
